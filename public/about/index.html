About I4C 2026

IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026

The IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026 is a two-day forum bringing together industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts, innovators, and students to explore emerging technologies and foster meaningful collaboration. The conclave focuses on technologies shaping the future of industry, including Electrification, Industrial Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, Smart Mobility, Advanced Manufacturing, and Industrial IoT. I4C 2026 provides a platform for participants to exchange knowledge, discover emerging trends, showcase innovative solutions, connect with experts, and explore opportunities for research, careers, innovation, and industry collaboration.

Hyderabad — The Host City

Experience Hyderabad

I4C 2026 takes place in Hyderabad, a major technology, innovation, research and industrial hub in India. Alongside the technical experience at the conclave, attendees can explore Hyderabad's rich heritage and popular landmarks, including the Charminar, Buddha Statue, Birla Mandir, and Nehru Zoological Park. The combination of technological innovation, academic engagement, industry interaction and Hyderabad's cultural heritage makes I4C 2026 an opportunity to learn, connect, collaborate, and innovate.
