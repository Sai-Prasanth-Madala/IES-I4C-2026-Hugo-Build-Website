IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026

ADVANCING ELECTRIFICATION, AUTOMATION, AI, RENEWABLE ENERGY AND SMART MOBILITY

Welcome to the IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026, a two-day technology and innovation forum organized by the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES) Hyderabad Hubs & Nodes.

The conclave brings together industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts, innovators, and students to explore emerging technologies, exchange knowledge, showcase innovative solutions, and build meaningful professional and research collaborations.

With a focus on the technologies transforming modern industries, I4C 2026 provides a platform to discuss practical challenges, emerging opportunities, and future directions across electrification, automation, artificial intelligence, renewable energy, and smart mobility.

What to Expect

The conclave features a combination of expert talks, interactive workshops, technology showcases, networking opportunities, and knowledge-sharing sessions designed to connect ideas with real-world industrial applications.

Key Highlights

  • Expert Talks and Keynotes
  • Industry-Academia Interaction
  • Technology Exhibition and Live Demonstrations
  • Hands-on Workshops
  • Speed Mentoring
  • IES Connect
  • Panel Discussions
  • Networking with Industry Leaders
  • Internship & Career Opportunities

Focus Areas

The IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026 will explore a broad range of technologies shaping the future of industry, including:

Areas of Innovation

  • Smart Manufacturing & Industry 5.0
  • Intelligent Power Electronics & Industrial Drives
  • Electric Vehicles & Smart Mobility
  • Battery Technologies & Charging
  • Renewable Energy & Smart Grid
  • Hydrogen & Fuel Cell Technologies
  • Artificial Intelligence for Industry
  • Digital Twins

Connect · Collaborate · Innovate

I4C 2026 is more than a technical gathering. It is a platform for connecting people, exchanging ideas, discovering opportunities, and building collaborations that can contribute to the next generation of industrial innovation.

Whether you are a student, researcher, academician, industry professional, entrepreneur, or technology enthusiast, the conclave offers an opportunity to learn from experts, showcase ideas, and engage with a community working toward a smarter and more sustainable industrial future.

We look forward to welcoming you to Hyderabad for I4C 2026.
