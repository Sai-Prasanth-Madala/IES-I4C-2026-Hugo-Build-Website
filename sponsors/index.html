IEEE Industrial Electronics Society

Event Sponsor

The IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES) is a technical society of IEEE dedicated to advancing the theory and application of electronics, controls, communications, instrumentation, and computational intelligence to industrial and manufacturing systems.

IES brings together engineers, researchers, academicians, students, and industry professionals working across areas including industrial automation, robotics, intelligent control, power electronics, industrial communications, smart grids, and related technologies.

Through its global technical community, IES promotes technological innovation, professional development, knowledge exchange, research, and collaboration between academia and industry.

The IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026 is organized through the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society Hyderabad Hubs & Nodes, creating a platform for students, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to explore emerging technologies and industrial applications.

As the Event Sponsor, IEEE IES supports the I4C 2026 initiative and its objective of bringing the industrial electronics and technology community together.

IEEE

Goodies Sponsor

IEEE is a global technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE brings together engineers, researchers, educators, students, and technology professionals through its technical communities, conferences, publications, standards, educational activities, and professional development opportunities.

The organization provides a global environment for knowledge exchange, technical collaboration, innovation, and professional growth across engineering, computing, electronics, and related fields.

For I4C 2026, IEEE supports the event as a Goodies Sponsor, contributing to the participant experience through sponsorship of event goodies.

The sponsorship also reflects the wider IEEE ecosystem supporting the conclave and its objective of connecting students, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators.

IEEE Collabratec

Goodies Sponsor

IEEE Collabratec is an IEEE professional networking, productivity, and collaboration platform created for engineers, technologists, researchers, and other technology professionals.

The platform enables members of the technical community to discover, connect, and collaborate through professional networks, technical communities, discussions, workspaces, and opportunities for knowledge exchange.

IEEE Collabratec supports activities such as:

  • Connecting with technology professionals and researchers
  • Participating in technical communities and discussions
  • Finding and connecting with subject-matter experts
  • Collaborating on technical projects and research
  • Building professional networks
  • Sharing technical knowledge and ideas
  • Discovering professional and volunteering opportunities

For I4C 2026, IEEE Collabratec supports the event as a Goodies Sponsor, contributing to the participant experience through sponsorship of event goodies.

Its focus on networking and collaboration aligns with the I4C 2026 vision of bringing together the IEEE community, academia, industry, researchers, students, and innovators.

IEEE Puzzlers

Goodies Sponsor

IEEE Puzzlers is an IEEE community focused on mathematical brain-teasers, puzzles, and problem-solving challenges.

The community encourages participants to engage in challenging and enjoyable problem-solving activities while developing analytical thinking and logical reasoning skills.

IEEE Puzzlers provides opportunities for participants to:

  • Solve mathematical and logical brain-teasers
  • Develop analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Challenge themselves with puzzles of different difficulty levels
  • Participate in an engaging IEEE community
  • Earn recognition through achievements and badges
  • Connect with other members who enjoy problem-solving

For I4C 2026, IEEE Puzzlers supports the event as a Goodies Sponsor, contributing to the participant experience through sponsorship of event goodies.

The community’s emphasis on analytical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving complements the innovation-oriented environment of the IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave.

The IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026 brings together students, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, engineers, innovators, and technology enthusiasts for two days of technical sessions, industry interaction, workshops, networking, and technology exploration.
