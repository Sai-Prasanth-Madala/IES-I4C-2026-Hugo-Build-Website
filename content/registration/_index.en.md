# Register for I4C 2026

The **IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026** brings together students, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, innovators and technology enthusiasts for two days of expert talks, industry interaction, workshops, networking and technology showcases.

📅 **22–23 September 2026**  
📍 **Engineering Staff College of India (ESCI), Gachibowli, Hyderabad**

---

# 🎟️ Zero Registration Fee

## Registration is completely FREE!

There is **no registration fee** to participate in the **IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026**.

All interested participants are invited to complete the official **Willingness / Expression of Interest Form**.

# What Can You Experience?

### 🎤 Expert Talks & Keynotes

Hear from distinguished experts from academia and industry on emerging technologies and industrial innovation.

### 🤝 Industry–Academia Interaction

Interact with professionals, researchers and academicians and explore opportunities for collaboration and knowledge exchange.

### 🏭 Technology Exhibition & Industry Expo

Explore technologies, products, innovations and live demonstrations at the **Technology Exhibition & Industry Expo**, which will remain open throughout the conference.

### 🛠️ Hands-on Workshops

Take part in practical learning experiences connecting technical concepts with real-world industrial applications.

### 💬 Panel Discussions

Engage with diverse perspectives on Artificial Intelligence, industrial engineering and the future of technology.

### 🚀 Speed Mentoring & Networking

Connect with experts, professionals, researchers and fellow participants through networking and speed mentoring activities.
