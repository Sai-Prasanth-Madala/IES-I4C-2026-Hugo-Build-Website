## IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026

The **IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026** brings together industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts and students for two days of technical sessions, industry interaction, networking and hands-on learning.

The programme covers key areas including **Artificial Intelligence, Power Electronics, Electric Vehicles, Green Hydrogen, Industrial Electronics, Digital Twins, Smart Manufacturing, Industry 5.0, RISC-V, FPGA-based computing and intelligent industrial systems**.
