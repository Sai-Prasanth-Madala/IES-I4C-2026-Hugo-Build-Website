# About IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026

The **IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026** is a two-day forum bringing together **industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts, innovators, and students** to explore emerging technologies and foster meaningful collaboration.

The conclave focuses on technologies shaping the future of industry, including **Electrification, Industrial Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, Smart Mobility, Advanced Manufacturing, and Industrial IoT**.

I4C 2026 provides a platform for participants to exchange knowledge, discover emerging trends, showcase innovative solutions, connect with experts, and explore opportunities for **research, careers, innovation, and industry collaboration**.

# Event Details

- **Event:** IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026
- **Date:** 22–23 September 2026
- **Venue:** Engineering Staff College of India (ESCI), Gachibowli, Hyderabad
- **Format:** Talks · Workshops · Expo
- **Organized by:** IEEE Industrial Electronics Society Hyderabad Hubs & Nodes

# What to Expect

I4C 2026 brings together diverse perspectives through:

- **Expert Talks & Keynotes**
- **Industry Talks**
- **Hands-on Workshops**
- **Industry–Academia Interaction**
- **Technology Exhibition & Industry Expo**
- **Panel Discussions**
- **Networking & Speed Mentoring**
- **Opportunities for Collaboration and Knowledge Exchange**

# Technology & Innovation Focus

The conclave explores emerging technologies and industrial applications across areas such as:

- **Artificial Intelligence & Industry 5.0**
- **Electrification & Intelligent Industrial Drives**
- **Electric Vehicles & Smart Mobility**
- **Battery Technologies & Charging**
- **Renewable Energy & Smart Grids**
- **Green Hydrogen & Fuel Cells**
- **Digital Twins & Industrial Automation**
- **Advanced Manufacturing**
- **Industrial IoT**
- **RISC-V, FPGA & Edge AI**

# Hyderabad — The Host City

I4C 2026 takes place in **Hyderabad**, a major technology, innovation, research, and industrial hub in India.

Alongside the technical experience at the conclave, attendees can explore Hyderabad's rich heritage and popular landmarks, including the **Charminar, Buddha Statue, Birla Mandir, and Nehru Zoological Park**.

The combination of technological innovation, academic engagement, industry interaction, and Hyderabad's cultural heritage makes I4C 2026 an opportunity to **learn, connect, collaborate, and innovate**.

# Connect · Collaborate · Innovate

I4C 2026 aims to create meaningful connections between **academia, industry, researchers, professionals, innovators, and students** while encouraging innovation, knowledge exchange, and future collaboration.

We look forward to welcoming you to **Hyderabad for IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026**.
