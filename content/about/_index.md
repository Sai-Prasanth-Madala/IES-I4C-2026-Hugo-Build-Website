## About IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026

The **IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026** is a two-day forum bringing together industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts, innovators, and students to explore emerging technologies and foster meaningful collaboration.

The conclave focuses on technologies shaping the future of industry, including **Electrification, Industrial Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, Smart Mobility, Advanced Manufacturing, and Industrial IoT**.

I4C 2026 provides a platform for participants to exchange knowledge, discover emerging trends, showcase innovative solutions, connect with experts, and explore opportunities for research and industry collaboration.

### Event Details

- **Event:** IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026
- **Date:** 22–23 September 2026
- **Venue:** Engineering Staff College of India (ESCI), Gachibowli, Hyderabad
- **Format:** Talks · Workshops · Expo
- **Organized by:** IEEE Industrial Electronics Society Hyderabad Hubs & Nodes

### Connect · Collaborate · Innovate

I4C 2026 aims to create meaningful connections between academia, industry, researchers, professionals, and students while encouraging innovation and knowledge exchange.
