## About I4C 2026

{{< card title="IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave 2026" >}}

The <strong>IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026</strong> is a two-day forum bringing together <strong>industry leaders, researchers, academicians, technology experts, innovators, and students</strong> to explore emerging technologies and foster meaningful collaboration.

The conclave focuses on technologies shaping the future of industry, including <strong>Electrification, Industrial Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Renewable Energy, Smart Mobility, Advanced Manufacturing, and Industrial IoT</strong>.

I4C 2026 provides a platform for participants to exchange knowledge, discover emerging trends, showcase innovative solutions, connect with experts, and explore opportunities for <strong>research, careers, innovation, and industry collaboration</strong>.

## Hyderabad — The Host City

{{< card title="Experience Hyderabad" >}}

I4C 2026 takes place in <strong>Hyderabad</strong>, a major technology, innovation, research and industrial hub in India.

Alongside the technical experience at the conclave, attendees can explore Hyderabad's rich heritage and popular landmarks, including the <strong>Charminar, Buddha Statue, Birla Mandir, and Nehru Zoological Park</strong>.

The combination of technological innovation, academic engagement, industry interaction and Hyderabad's cultural heritage makes I4C 2026 an opportunity to <strong>learn, connect, collaborate, and innovate</strong>.
