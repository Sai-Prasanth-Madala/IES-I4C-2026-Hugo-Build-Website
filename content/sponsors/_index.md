{{< featureCard title="IEEE Industrial Electronics Society" >}}

![IEEE Industrial Electronics Society](/images/footer/sponsors/IEEE-IES.png "IEEE Industrial Electronics Society")

<strong>Event Sponsor</strong>

The <strong>IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (IES)</strong> is a technical society of IEEE dedicated to advancing the theory and application of electronics, controls, communications, instrumentation, and computational intelligence to industrial and manufacturing systems.

IES brings together engineers, researchers, academicians, students, and industry professionals working across areas including industrial automation, robotics, intelligent control, power electronics, industrial communications, smart grids, and related technologies.

Through its global technical community, IES promotes technological innovation, professional development, knowledge exchange, research, and collaboration between academia and industry.

The <strong>IEEE IES Industrial Innovation Conclave (I4C) 2026</strong> is organized through the <strong>IEEE Industrial Electronics Society Hyderabad Hubs & Nodes</strong>, creating a platform for students, researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to explore emerging technologies and industrial applications.

As the <strong>Event Sponsor</strong>, IEEE IES supports the I4C 2026 initiative and its objective of bringing the industrial electronics and technology community together.
